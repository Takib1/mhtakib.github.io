§ 01 — About

A practitioner moving toward theory

Statement
of Interest

I am an Industrial and Production Engineering graduate from the Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST), Bangladesh, currently serving as a Supply Service Executive at Reckitt Benckiser Bangladesh. Over the past three years I have managed the import network for ninety-four SKUs across twelve international suppliers — and watched the limits of the heuristics I rely on every day.

That gap between what works in practice and what can be proven optimal is what draws me toward doctoral research. My interests sit at the intersection of mixed-integer and network optimization, stochastic and distributionally robust programming, and data-driven decision modeling — areas where the mathematical work has direct consequence for the kinds of supplier-selection, inventory, and routing problems I face professionally.

I am applying for Spring and Fall 2027 admission to PhD programs in Industrial Engineering, Operations Research, and Systems Engineering, and I welcome conversation with faculty whose work intersects these areas.

§ 02 — Research

Where I want to go deeper

01

Mixed-Integer & Network Optimization

Strengthened formulations for large-scale supplier selection, facility location, and routing problems where combinatorial structure makes naive models intractable.

02

Stochastic & Distributionally Robust Optimization

Decision-making under demand and supply uncertainty — particularly two-stage and multi-stage frameworks where the distribution itself is uncertain.

03

Optimization Under Uncertainty in Large-Scale Systems

Decomposition algorithms — Benders, Lagrangian, column generation — that make industrial-scale stochastic problems computationally tractable.

04

Data-Driven Decision Modeling

Integration of machine learning with optimization for prescriptive analytics — moving from forecast-then-optimize toward jointly learned policies.
